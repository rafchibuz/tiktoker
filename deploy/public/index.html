TWITCH CLIP EDITOR

From a live moment to a vertical video.

Tiktoker helps creators discover Twitch clips, turn selected moments into vertical videos, review captions and framing, and prepare an approved post for TikTok.

Open studio

How it works

01 · Discover

Monitor selected Twitch channels or analyze a past stream. Clips remain separate so the creator chooses which moment to use.

02 · Edit

Approve a clip, adjust framing and duration, edit subtitles, and render a 1080×1920 MP4.

03 · Review

Check the finished video, description, and cover frame before approving a specific version for publishing.

Creator controls

Channel-specific video and description templates save repetitive work. The creator can change or reject each result. TikTok account connection and Direct Post are being integrated; no post is sent without an explicit approval in the studio.
