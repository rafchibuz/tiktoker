Privacy Policy

Effective 15 November 2026. Privacy contact: mark.vip11174@gmail.com.

Data we use

Tiktoker processes Twitch channel and clip metadata, selected video files, editing settings, generated subtitles, descriptions, and review decisions. If you authorize TikTok access, we process the TikTok account identifier and the access and refresh tokens needed to publish approved posts. The studio also receives ordinary connection data such as IP addresses and request timestamps.

Why and where

We use this data to provide clip discovery, video editing, account connection, approved publishing, error diagnosis, and service security. Files and application records are stored on infrastructure operated for Tiktoker. Twitch and TikTok receive data necessary for their respective integrations. We do not sell personal data.

Access and retention

Studio access is restricted by login. Tokens and application secrets are not exposed in public web pages or the source repository. We retain project records while the account is active or as needed to provide the service; you may ask us to delete your account, media, and associated records. Backups and operational logs may take additional time to clear.

Your choices

You can decline TikTok authorization and continue using available editing tools. You can revoke TikTok access in TikTok settings. For access, correction, deletion, or questions, email mark.vip11174@gmail.com. We will review requests according to applicable law.

Updates

We will post changes to this policy here and update its effective date.
